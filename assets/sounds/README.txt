Place your ambient sound files here.

Supported formats: .mp3, .wav, .ogg, .flac, .m4a

The file name (without extension) becomes the display name in the app.
Underscores and dashes are converted to spaces.

Examples:
  rain.mp3          → "Rain"
  ocean_waves.wav   → "Ocean Waves"
  coffee-shop.ogg   → "Coffee Shop"
  forest_birds.flac → "Forest Birds"

After adding or removing files, click the "Refresh" button
on the Music page (or restart the app).
